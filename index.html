Rooted in tradition. Defined by versatility.

We began under the high tents of Oktoberfest and promptly refused to stay in our lane. Today we field our core quintet and contract custom ensembles from Portland’s finest freelance talent.

01 — Signature

The Polka Quintet

Trumpet, clarinet, trombone, banjo and drums. Our flagship band for beer gardens, festivals and anywhere that needs a joyful, roving sound.

Ideal for: Oktoberfest, fairs, parties

02 — Ceremony

Chamber & Solo Trumpet

Refined processionals and preludes, from a single trumpet to a small chamber group, tailored to your venue and program.

Ideal for: Weddings, memorials, galas

03 — Contracting

Custom Ensembles

Need a jazz combo for cocktail hour or a holiday brass quartet? We assemble Portland’s finest freelancers and handle every logistic.

Ideal for: Corporate & private events
